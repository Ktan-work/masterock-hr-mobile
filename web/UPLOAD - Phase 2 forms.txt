Central HRA web app - Phase 2 (Forms) upload steps
==================================================

1. Open https://github.com/Ktan-work/masterock-hr-mobile/upload/main
2. From this folder (HR Automation App\web), drag BOTH of these onto the upload page:
     - hra.html                (replaces the current one)
     - the whole "hra-assets" folder   (drag the folder itself, so its subfolders pcforms and claimforms come along)
3. Click "Commit changes".
4. Wait about 1-2 minutes, then open https://ktan-work.github.io/masterock-hr-mobile/hra.html
   (press Ctrl+F5 once so the browser loads the new version).

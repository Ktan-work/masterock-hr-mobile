Phone sign-in fix (Masterock) + new Whiterain HRA web app - setup steps
=========================================================================

STEP 1 - allow the phone sign-in (one time, Google Cloud)
  1. Open https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/clients?project=masterock-hr-514313  (signed in as ktan6689)
  2. Click the web client "Masterock HR Mobile".
  3. Under "Authorized redirect URIs" click "+ Add URI" twice and enter:
       https://ktan-work.github.io/masterock-hr-mobile/web/
       https://ktan-work.github.io/masterock-hr-mobile/web/whiterain/
  4. Click Save. (Takes up to 5 minutes to work.)

STEP 2 - upload
  1. Open https://github.com/Ktan-work/masterock-hr-mobile/upload/main/web
  2. From HR Automation App\web drag in: index.html, hra.html and the whole "whiterain" folder.
  3. Commit changes. Wait 1-2 minutes.
  Links:  Masterock  https://ktan-work.github.io/masterock-hr-mobile/web/
          Whiterain  https://ktan-work.github.io/masterock-hr-mobile/web/whiterain/

STEP 3 - give someone access (repeat per person)
  a. Google Cloud: https://console.cloud.google.com/auth/audience?project=masterock-hr-514313
     -> "Test users" -> "+ Add users" -> their Gmail address -> Save.   (up to 100 people)
  b. Google Drive: share the database with them as Editor:
       Masterock team -> "Masterock HR Database"
       Whiterain team -> "Whiterain HR Database" (Drive folder "Whiterain HR")
     Salary group only -> also share the matching "... Compensation (restricted)" sheet.
  c. Send them the link. Each time they sign in, a 4-digit PIN is emailed to you -
     pass it to them only if you approve.

On phones: open the link in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone), not inside Telegram/Messenger.

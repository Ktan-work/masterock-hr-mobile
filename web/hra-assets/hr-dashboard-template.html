🍰 14 October — National Dessert Day. Celebrated each year on 14 October, National Dessert Day is quite literally a day dedicated to indulging your sweet tooth — with cakes, cookies, ice cream, pastries and every sugary delight in between. From a childhood favourite to an elaborate patisserie creation, desserts bring a little joy and sweetness to the everyday.
